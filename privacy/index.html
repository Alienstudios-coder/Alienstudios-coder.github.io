Privacy notice

Short version: this website does not collect, store or share your personal information.

Who we are

Alien Studios is a software development studio based in Kasarani, Nairobi, Kenya. For any privacy question, contact Paul Muthoni at paulmuthoni88@gmail.com or 0729 927 670.

What this website collects

  • No cookies and no local tracking identifiers. The only thing stored in your browser is your light/dark theme choice, if you change it.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served from this website, so your visit is not shared with other companies.
  • The demo booking form does not send anything to this website. It prepares a message that opens in your own WhatsApp or email app; you decide whether to send it.

The hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you message, email or call us, we use your details only to reply, arrange a demonstration and prepare a quotation. We do not sell or share them. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to its use. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Client systems

The management systems we build for clients run on the client's own computers. Each client is the data controller for the records in its system; data storage, access and backups are documented with every client before installation.
