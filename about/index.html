Alien Studios is a software development studio in Nairobi. We build management systems for organizations that want less paperwork, fewer errors and clearer information for decision-makers.

Our story

Started by a developer who listens first

Alien Studios began as a design studio — logos, flyers, posters and websites for local businesses. Working closely with schools, clinics and offices, one thing kept coming up: the real pain was not how their documents looked, but how much time their teams lost to paper registers, spreadsheets and manual calculations.

So we started building the systems behind the paperwork. Today the studio has six working management systems — for driving schools, high schools, primary schools, hospitals, uniformed units and HR & payroll — and builds custom systems for any organization with a process worth organizing.

We still do design. It means the same team can build your system, your website and the brochure that explains it.

What we believe

Principles we build by

01

Your workflow first

We learn how you work before we write a line of code — then configure the system to match.

02

Honest demonstrations

We only show what is built. Every demo uses fictitious data, and nothing is promised that the software cannot do.

03

Your data, your premises

Data stays on computers you control, backed up automatically and exportable at any time.

04

Security is not optional

Roles, audit logs, lockouts and data-protection documentation come standard, not as extras.

05

Built for local realities

Unreliable internet, M-Pesa, KRA, NSSF, SHIF, CBC and MoH reporting are designed in from the start.

06

Clear, written terms

Scope, costs, timelines and support are agreed in writing before work begins.
