Questions

Frequently asked

Is the demonstration really free?

Yes. We bring a working demonstration with fictitious data to your office in Nairobi, or show it online. There is no obligation to buy.

Do your systems need internet?

No. They run on a computer you own, on your own network, and keep working when the internet is down. Only optional features such as live M-Pesa payments need internet on the server computer.

Where is our data stored?

On your premises, on a computer you control. Backups are made automatically and your data can be exported to Excel or CSV at any time.

Can the system follow our own rules?

Yes. Approval chains, leave rules, fee structures, roles and reports are configured to how your organization already works. Larger changes are quoted in writing.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the system and the data to migrate. Ready-built systems can usually be installed and staff trained within days; the timeline is agreed in writing in your quotation.

Do you provide training and support?

Yes. Every installation includes training for each group of users, a written guide and a demo copy to practise on. Ongoing support is provided under a clear support agreement.
