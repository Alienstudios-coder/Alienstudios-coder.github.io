Why Alien Studios

Engineered for the realities of Kenyan organizations

Unreliable internet, M-Pesa, KRA and NSSF, CBC, MoH returns, the Data Protection Act — we design for them from the first line of code.

Online or fully offline

Our systems run on a computer you own, on your own network. Staff use any browser — no installation on their devices — and work never stops when the internet does.

Server PC

Secure by default

Personal logins, hashed passwords, lockout, automatic sign-out, role-based access and an audit log of every important action.

M-Pesa built in

STK Push payments confirmed with Safaricom and recorded automatically — no retyping receipt codes.

Your data stays yours

Stored on your premises, backed up automatically, exportable to Excel at any time. No lock-in.

Kenyan context

PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy. CBC levels. MOH 705 and IDSR. Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Built around how you already work

Your approval chain, your leave rules, your fee structure, your reports. We start by listening, then configure the system to your process — not the other way round.
