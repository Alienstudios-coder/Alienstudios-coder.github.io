Inside the system

Everything in one controlled record

Every feature listed here exists in the current build and can be shown in a live demonstration with fictitious data.

See it live
  • Student registration with course, fee, status and photo; a portal account is created automatically (username = phone number)
  • Course catalogue with default fees and number of classes
  • Lesson booking by date, time and duration with instructor, student and vehicle clash checks
  • Lesson requests from the student portal, approved or declined by reception
  • Instructor records and document uploads (PDF/JPG/PNG) checked by content, with expiry tracking
  • Vehicle register with photos, status and next service date
  • Office payments (cash, bank, M-Pesa) and M-Pesa STK Push from the portal, recorded once and auto-verified
  • Assessments with scores and skills passed
  • Dashboards, course reports, CSV export and an activity log

Technical summary

Runs on your premises. Your data stays yours.

Installed on a computer you own, used from any browser on your network. Personal logins, role-based access, an audit log and automatic backups come as standard.
